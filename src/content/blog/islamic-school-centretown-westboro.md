Searching for an Islamic school in downtown Ottawa? There is no full-time Islamic elementary school in Centretown, Westboro, Little Italy or Hintonburg that we could verify as of October 2026. The nearest full-day Islamic kindergarten is closer than most downtown parents expect. QAF School at 900 Dynes Rd is about 4–7 km from these neighbourhoods, roughly 6–15 minutes by car outside rush hour, straight down Preston St and Prince of Wales Dr. For older children, two downtown masjids run weekend and youth classes.

Here is what downtown and west-central families can realistically reach, by car, by transit and on foot.

> **Key takeaways**
> - Little Italy is about 4 km from QAF; Hintonburg about 6 km; Centretown and Westboro about 7 km.
> - The morning drive runs south, away from downtown, along Prince of Wales Dr past the Central Experimental Farm.
> - Centretown Mosque (397 Kent St) offers a kids' program for ages 6–16 covering hifz, Arabic and Islamic Studies.
> - The Ottawa Main Mosque on Northwestern Ave, near Westboro, hosts OMA Sunday School on Sunday mornings.
> - Board schools downtown are walkable and free. A full-day Islamic JK/SK is the alternative when you want Quran and Arabic in every school day.

## Masjids and weekend learning near downtown

Downtown families have two well-established masjids with structured children's learning.

**Centretown Mosque, 397 Kent St.** This 1911 building, once the First United Church and later the Ottawa Chinese-Canadian Heritage Centre, became the Centretown Mosque in 2025. According to an [IQRA profile](https://www.iqra.ca/2025/ottawas-centretown-mosque-a-new-chapter-for-a-historic-ottawa-landmark/), it runs a Kids Education program for ages 6 to 16 covering hifz (Quran memorization), Arabic and Islamic Studies. It also offers adult Quran and fiqh classes. Ask the mosque directly for current days and fees.

**Ottawa Main Mosque, 251 Northwestern Ave.** Ottawa's oldest mosque, run by the [Ottawa Muslim Association](https://ottawamosque.ca), sits in Champlain Park, a few minutes north of Westboro. Its [OMA Sunday School](https://ss.ottawamosque.ca/) meets on Sundays from 9:30 AM to 1:00 PM and teaches the fundamentals of Islamic faith and moral principles.

The city has many other weekend programs. Our guide to [weekend Islamic schools in Ottawa](/blog/weekend-islamic-schools-ottawa) lists them by area, with fees where published.

## The commute: a reverse drive down Prince of Wales

Most morning traffic heads into downtown. Driving from Centretown or Little Italy to Dynes Rd, you head the other way: south on Preston St or Bronson Ave, then along Prince of Wales Dr past the Central Experimental Farm and Dow's Lake. QAF is just off Prince of Wales Dr in Carleton Heights.

Route notes by neighbourhood:

- **Little Italy and Dow's Lake:** Preston St to Prince of Wales Dr is the shortest run in this guide, about 4 km.
- **Hintonburg:** Somerset St W east to Preston, then south. About 6 km.
- **Centretown:** Bronson Ave south to Carling Ave, then Prince of Wales Dr. About 7 km.
- **Westboro:** Churchill Ave to Carling Ave, then south on Fisher Ave and across Baseline Rd. About 7 km.

For QAF's 8:45–9:00 AM drop-off window, leaving downtown by about 8:30 AM is usually comfortable. Pick-up runs 3:00–3:15 PM, before the evening rush builds on Prince of Wales Dr.

## No car? Transit and walking options

Many downtown families do not own a car, so here is an honest picture.

The O-Train Line 2 runs north–south through this area, with stations at Bayview, Corso Italia, Dow's Lake, Carleton and Mooney's Bay. It does not stop right at QAF, which sits on the west side of the Rideau River. A daily transit trip with a four-year-old would mean a train plus a bus or a walk. Plan it on [OC Transpo's trip planner](https://www.octranspo.com/en/) and try it once on a school-day morning before you rely on it.

For car-free families, a carpool with another downtown family or a car-share for the short drive is often the practical answer. For weekend programs, the Centretown Mosque is walkable from much of Centretown. The Ottawa Main Mosque is a short bus or bike ride from Westboro and Hintonburg.

## Downtown board schools: what you get

Every downtown address has a designated school in each of Ottawa's four boards. Use the [OCDSB school locator](https://www.ocdsb.ca/our_schools/school_locator) and the OCSB's locator to find yours. Check again before registering, because the OCDSB has been consulting on new elementary attendance boundaries.

What downtown board schools offer:

- **Free, full-day JK and SK.** In the English boards, kindergarten is about half English and half French. From Grade 1, families choose English with Core French or Early French Immersion; see [French Immersion vs English in Ottawa](/blog/french-immersion-vs-english-ottawa).
- **French-language schools.** CEPEO and CECCE schools are an option if you are eligible, which mainly depends on the parents' own French-language rights.
- **Walkability.** For many downtown families, this is the strongest argument for the local school.

What they do not include is Quran, Arabic, or daily duas and adab. Downtown families who go the board-school route usually add a weekend or after-school Quran program.

## What a full-day Islamic kindergarten adds for city kids

[Our Islamic JK/SK program](/program) is built for ages 4 and 5, and a few things matter especially to downtown families:

- **Space to move and focus.** Children who live in apartments and condos benefit from a Montessori-inspired classroom with a long, uninterrupted morning work cycle (9:20–11:00) and room to work with their hands.
- **Arabic every day.** Daily Arabic immersion alongside English, during the years children pick up language most easily.
- **Faith as routine.** Short surahs from Juz Amma, Islamic Studies, duas and adab are part of the day, not an extra.
- **Ontario-aligned.** The program is aligned with Ontario's kindergarten curriculum, so children are ready for Grade 1 anywhere.
- **Practical details.** The school day runs 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday to Friday, September to June. Tuition is $690 per month for 10 months plus a $190 resource fee ($7,090 a year), with a sibling discount. Families pack a halal, nut-free lunch and two snacks, and parents follow the day on ClassDojo.

## Older kids: picking a Sunday

For ages 8 to 15, downtown families have a real choice of Sunday programs, and the schedules overlap. OMA Sunday School runs 9:30 AM to 1:00 PM. [QAF Sunday Camp](/sunday-camp) runs 11 AM to 3 PM at 900 Dynes Rd. You can do one or the other, not both.

QAF Sunday Camp suits children who need Quran and Arabic reading and writing alongside math and STEM, plus soccer to burn off energy. It costs $190 a month from September to June, with junior and senior classes. If your child mainly needs hifz with a teacher close to home, the Centretown Mosque's kids' program may be the better fit. Compare more options in our guide to [Quran classes in Ottawa](/blog/quran-classes-for-kids-ottawa).

## Downtown family life that supports learning

City living has its own advantages for a young Muslim learner:

- **Libraries:** the Ottawa Public Library's Main branch on Metcalfe St and the Rosemount branch in Wellington West are easy places to borrow Muslim picture books and Arabic titles on hold.
- **Green space:** Dow's Lake, Commissioners Park and the Central Experimental Farm are all close to the Prince of Wales Dr route, which makes a good after-school stop.
- **Masjid habits:** walking to Maghrib at the Centretown Mosque, or Jumu'ah at the Ottawa Main Mosque on a PA day, links what children learn in class to community life.

## Is QAF right for your family?

QAF is a good fit for downtown, Little Italy, Hintonburg and Westboro families with a child aged 4 or 5, access to a car or carpool, and a wish for Quran, Arabic and Islamic manners in every school day. The short southbound drive is one of the easier commutes in the city.

It is probably not the right fit if walking to school is non-negotiable, if you rely only on transit, or if you want one school from JK to Grade 8; we offer JK and SK only. Children aged 6 and 7 are too young for our Sunday Camp, which starts at 8, so the Centretown Mosque program or another weekend school may suit them sooner. If you are in the Glebe or Old Ottawa South, the drive is likely similar in length. Compare it with the Hunt Club route in our [South Keys guide](/blog/islamic-school-south-keys).
