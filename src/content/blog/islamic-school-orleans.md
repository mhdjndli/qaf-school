## Weekday or weekend: which model fits your family?

There is no single right choice. These are the trade-offs Orléans parents describe most often.

**Full-time Islamic school** gives your child Quran, Arabic and Islamic Studies every day, with prayer built into the schedule and classmates who share the same faith. It costs tuition and, outside Orléans, it usually means a long daily drive. Jamia Islamia is the obvious local choice.

**Public or Catholic school plus weekday-evening Quran** keeps tuition at zero during the day and adds structured Quran two to four evenings a week. It works well if your evenings are predictable, but it can be tiring for young children after a full school day.

**Public school plus a weekend program** is the most common model in Ottawa. It gives children a longer, more relaxed block of Quran and Arabic when they are fresh, and it leaves weekday evenings free for homework and family time. The trade-off is less daily contact with Quran, so a short daily review at home matters.

If you are weighing the bigger question, our honest comparison of [Islamic school vs. public school in Ontario](/blog/islamic-school-vs-public-school-ontario) goes deeper.

## Masjid-based Quran programs in the east end

Orléans has two well-known masajid, and both play a big part in children’s Islamic education here.

**Masjid Bilal (Islamic Society of Cumberland), 4509 Innes Rd.** Close to Avalon and Fallingbrook. Masjid Bilal has run a Quran program for children with two tracks: weekday evenings (Monday to Thursday, 5:30 to 7:30 PM) and weekends (Saturday and Sunday, 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM), covering Quran reading, tajweed and memorization, with students placed by level. Contact the Islamic Society of Cumberland to confirm this year’s days, ages and fees.

**Darul Uloom Ottawa, 2803 St. Joseph Blvd.** A masjid and centre on St. Joseph Blvd in central Orléans. It has publicly fundraised with the goal of establishing Islamic elementary and secondary schooling, so it is worth asking what classes for children it currently offers.

When you compare Quran programs, ask how students are grouped (by reading level, not just age), how progress is shared with parents and how much home review is expected. Our guide to [Quran classes for kids in Ottawa](/blog/quran-classes-for-kids-ottawa) has a full checklist.

## Free Saturday Arabic in Orléans

Many parents do not know that Ottawa’s English school boards teach Arabic for free on Saturday mornings, and both have an Orléans site.

- **OCDSB at Fallingbrook Community Elementary School, 679 Deancourt Cres.** Saturdays from 9:30 to 12:00, September 12, 2026 to June 19, 2027. Students from any board in JK to Grade 8 can join; Ontario residents pay a $20 activity fee. Register through the [OCDSB International Languages program](https://www.ocdsb.ca/programs-learning/continuing-education/international-languages-program/international-indigenous-languages-elementary-program).
- **OCSB at St. Matthew High School, 6550 Bilberry Dr.** Saturdays from 9:30 to 12:00 for students entering JK to Grade 8, with a $10 materials fee. Registration is open until March 2027 on the [OCSB Continuing Education site](https://conted.ocsb.ca/elementary/international-languages-elementary/).

These classes teach Arabic as a language, which makes Quran reading easier later. For families who want Quran and Islamic Studies too, they pair well with a masjid program. Our guide to [Arabic classes for kids in Ottawa](/blog/arabic-classes-for-kids-ottawa) explains how to combine them. **Dar-Ul-Huda School**, run by the Association of Islamic Charitable Projects, is another Saturday option for children from age 5, with Arabic and Quran classes from 9:00 AM to 1:30 PM. Confirm the location with [AICP](https://www.aicp.ca/activities-ottawa-ontario/) before your first visit.

## Cumberland and Navan families

If you live in Cumberland village or Navan, your closest options are still in Orléans. Jamia Islamia is about 9 minutes from Cumberland, and Masjid Bilal on Innes Rd is likely the nearest masjid for many Navan families. The Saturday Arabic sites at Fallingbrook and St. Matthew are roughly 10 to 20 minutes away.

For Navan in particular, distances to anything west of Orléans add up quickly: QAF is about 29 km from Navan, roughly 35 minutes outside rush hour. A local weekday option plus a weekend program closer to home is usually the practical route. Many Orléans families are also francophone or bilingual; our French-language guide, [École islamique à Ottawa](/blog/ecole-islamique-ottawa), may be useful if French is part of your family’s plan.

## How far is QAF from Orléans?

QAF is at 900 Dynes Rd in Nepean, just off Prince of Wales Dr. Here are typical Sunday-morning drives, when traffic is light. Weekday rush-hour drives are much longer.

| Starting point | Approx. distance | Typical Sunday-morning drive |
|---|---|---|
| Convent Glen, Orléans West | 22 km | About 22–28 minutes |
| Central Orléans (Place d’Orléans area) | 24 km | About 25–30 minutes |
| Avalon | 25 km | About 28–33 minutes |
| Cumberland village | 32 km | About 30–35 minutes |
| Navan | 29 km | About 34–40 minutes |

The main route is Highway 174 to Highway 417 west, then south toward Prince of Wales Dr. For a full-day kindergarten five days a week, that is a long round trip, and we would not recommend it for most families. For a once-a-week Sunday program, many families find it manageable, especially if they combine it with errands or a visit to family in the west.

## Is QAF Sunday Camp right for your Orléans family?

QAF Sunday Camp can be a good fit if your child is 8 to 15, attends public, Catholic or French-language school during the week, and you want one longer, well-rounded block of Islamic learning each week. It runs every Sunday from 11 AM to 3 PM at 900 Dynes Rd, in junior and senior classes. The day combines Quran in small, level-based groups with Arabic reading and writing, math and STEM, and soccer. Tuition is $190 a month from September to June. QAF also runs Quran halaqat with a parent portal that shows attendance and hifz progress, which helps parents follow progress from across the city.

It is probably not the right fit if a 25- to 35-minute drive each Sunday is too much, or if your child already attends a weekend program at Masjid Bilal or a Saturday Arabic class and is thriving. In that case, staying local is the better choice.
