Barrhaven families looking for an Islamic school have two realistic paths: SNMC Albushra School on Woodroffe Ave, inside Barrhaven itself, or one of the Nepean schools a short drive north up Prince of Wales Dr or Woodroffe Ave, including QAF’s Islamic JK and SK on Dynes Rd. This Islamic school Barrhaven guide compares both paths, with real commute routes, local mosques, free weekend Arabic classes and the details parents ask about most.

> **Key takeaways**
> - SNMC Albushra School at 3020 Woodroffe Ave is Barrhaven’s full-time Islamic school, offering JK to Grade 6.
> - QAF School at 900 Dynes Rd is about 12 to 18 km from most Barrhaven neighbourhoods, roughly 15 to 25 minutes by car outside rush hour.
> - Barrhaven has two major Islamic centres: SNMC on Woodroffe Ave and the MAC Barrhaven Islamic Centre on Greenbank Rd.
> - Free Saturday Arabic classes run at Farley Mowat Public School (OCDSB) and St. Mother Teresa High School (OCSB).

## Staying in Barrhaven or driving north?

This is the real decision most Barrhaven parents face, and there is no single right answer.

**Staying local makes sense if:** you want one school from JK to Grade 6, you value walking or a five-minute drive, or you want your child’s school and your masjid in the same place. Albushra shares its site with the SNMC centre, so school, Jumu’ah and community events overlap.

**Driving north makes sense if:** you are looking for a particular approach for the kindergarten years, such as a Montessori-inspired classroom or daily Arabic immersion, or if you want a school that runs to Grade 8 or Grade 12. Many Barrhaven parents already drive north every morning for work, so a stop in Nepean on the way is not a big detour.

A common pattern we see on tours is a hybrid one: a specialist Islamic kindergarten for JK and SK, then a school closer to home from Grade 1. If you choose that path, our checklist on [preparing your child for Grade 1](/blog/preparing-your-child-for-grade-1) helps with the switch.

## Commute routes from Barrhaven to Dynes Rd

Barrhaven connects to Nepean through three main north–south roads. Which one is fastest depends on where in Barrhaven you live. These estimates are for driving to 900 Dynes Rd outside rush hour; add time in heavy traffic and snow.

| Starting point | Approx. distance | Typical drive | Best route |
|---|---|---|---|
| Old Barrhaven (around Malvern Dr) | 13.5 km | About 17–22 minutes | Greenbank Rd or Woodroffe Ave to Fallowfield Rd, then Prince of Wales Dr |
| Longfields, Davidson Heights | 12 km | About 16–20 minutes | Woodroffe Ave to Fallowfield Rd, then Prince of Wales Dr |
| Chapman Mills, Barrhaven East | 13 km | About 18–22 minutes | Prince of Wales Dr north the whole way |
| Stonebridge | 16.5 km | About 20–25 minutes | Longfields Dr, then Prince of Wales Dr |
| Half Moon Bay | 18 km | About 22–28 minutes | Cambrian Rd and Longfields Dr, then Prince of Wales Dr |

**Prince of Wales Dr** is the simplest route for families in east Barrhaven: it runs north along the Rideau River and meets Dynes Rd near Hog’s Back, with few turns. **Woodroffe Ave** suits families in Longfields and Old Barrhaven heading to Fallowfield. **Greenbank Rd** is handy from the west but can be slow near Hunt Club.

QAF’s drop-off window is 8:45 to 9:00 AM and pick-up is 3:00 to 3:15 PM. If you work downtown or in central Nepean, Dynes Rd may already be close to your route north. Try the drive once at your real times before you decide. If you share the drive, ask the school whether other families from your area are open to carpooling.

## Barrhaven’s mosques and what they offer children

Barrhaven is one of the best-served parts of Ottawa for masajid, and both major centres run activities for children.

**SNMC (South Nepean Muslim Community), 3020 Woodroffe Ave.** One of the largest Muslim centres in Ottawa’s south end, and the home of SNMC Albushra School. SNMC holds two Jumu’ah prayers each Friday and runs a youth program with regular events for children. For example, it listed a free children’s evening for ages 5 to 13 this fall. See the [SNMC website](https://snmc.ca/) for current programs.

**Barrhaven Islamic Centre (MAC), 3971 Greenbank Rd.** A Muslim Association of Canada centre in west Barrhaven, close to Stonebridge and Half Moon Bay. When we checked, its site listed Quran halaqa classes for 2026–27 (with a waitlist), Arabic classes for adults, sports nights and family events. Details are on the [Barrhaven Islamic Centre page](https://centres.macnet.ca/bic/).

Qurtuba Islamic Centre on Merivale Rd is also about a 10- to 15-minute drive from Old Barrhaven.

## Weekend Arabic and Quran classes in Barrhaven

If your children attend public or Catholic school, weekends are where Quran and Arabic usually happen. Barrhaven has two free board-run options right in the neighbourhood.

- **OCDSB Arabic at Farley Mowat Public School, 75 Waterbridge Dr.** Saturday mornings from 9:30 to 12:00, September 12, 2026 to June 19, 2027. Open to JK to Grade 8 students from any board, with a $20 activity fee for Ontario residents. Register through the [OCDSB International Languages program](https://www.ocdsb.ca/programs-learning/continuing-education/international-languages-program/international-indigenous-languages-elementary-program).
- **OCSB Arabic at St. Mother Teresa High School, 440 Longfields Dr.** Saturday mornings from 9:30 to 12:00 for students entering JK to Grade 8, with a $10 materials fee. Registration stays open until March 2027. Details are on the [OCSB Continuing Education site](https://conted.ocsb.ca/elementary/international-languages-elementary/).

These classes teach Arabic as a language. For Quran reading, memorization and Islamic Studies, Barrhaven families usually add a masjid halaqa or a weekend program. Our guide to [Quran classes for kids in Ottawa](/blog/quran-classes-for-kids-ottawa) explains what to look for.

**QAF Sunday Camp** is another option for ages 8 to 15. It runs every Sunday from 11 AM to 3 PM at 900 Dynes Rd, combining Quran in small groups, Arabic reading and writing, math and STEM, and soccer. It costs $190 a month from September to June. Because it starts at 11 AM on a Sunday, the drive from Barrhaven is usually at the quick end of the ranges above. See the [Sunday Quran and Arabic camp in Ottawa](/sunday-camp).

## Public and Catholic schools in Barrhaven

Barrhaven has a large network of public schools, which many Muslim families use for some or all of their children’s schooling. All four Ottawa boards (OCDSB, OCSB and the two French boards, CEPEO and CECCE) have schools in the area, and each offers free full-day JK and SK.

Your designated school depends on your exact address, and new schools open as Barrhaven grows, so use the [OCDSB school locator](https://www.ocdsb.ca/our-schools/school-locator) or the OCSB’s school list to check. If you are comparing boards, our [Ottawa school boards guide](/blog/ottawa-school-boards-compared) explains how English, Catholic and French boards differ, and our post on [Islamic school vs. public school in Ontario](/blog/islamic-school-vs-public-school-ontario) covers the bigger choice honestly.

## A week for a Barrhaven family using both QAF programs

Here is how one realistic week can look for a Barrhaven family with a 4-year-old and a 9-year-old. It is an example to help you picture the logistics, not a requirement.

1. **Monday to Friday:** The 4-year-old leaves Barrhaven around 8:20 AM for QAF’s Islamic JK, arriving for the 8:45 to 9:00 drop-off. The day includes a long morning work cycle, daily Arabic immersion, short surahs and duas. Pick-up is 3:00 to 3:15 PM.
2. **Weekdays for the older child:** The 9-year-old attends the local public school in Barrhaven and does 10 minutes of Quran review at home after dinner.
3. **Saturday morning:** The 9-year-old joins the free OCDSB or OCSB Arabic class in Barrhaven.
4. **Sunday:** The 9-year-old goes to QAF Sunday Camp from 11 AM to 3 PM for Quran, Arabic, STEM and soccer.

Parents see the younger child’s day through photos, videos and messages on ClassDojo, so the commute feels less like a black box.

## Is QAF right for your Barrhaven family?

QAF is a strong fit if your child is 4 or 5, you are comfortable with a 15- to 25-minute drive, and you want a full-day Islamic kindergarten with a Montessori-inspired approach aligned with Ontario’s kindergarten curriculum. It also works well for families who already drive north for work. Tuition is $690 a month for 10 months (September to June) plus a $190 annual resource fee, with a sibling discount.

QAF is probably not right if you want to walk to school, if you need care outside 8:45 AM to 3:15 PM, or if you want one school from JK to Grade 6 or beyond. In that case, Albushra in Barrhaven or one of the K–8 schools in Nepean may be a better match. For older children, the [weekend Islamic schools in Ottawa](/blog/weekend-islamic-schools-ottawa) guide and our Sunday Camp are good places to start.
