There is no full-time Islamic school inside Riverside South, Findlay Creek or Manotick that we could verify as of October 2026, but several are within a 10- to 20-minute drive. Choosing an Islamic school Riverside South families can reach easily depends mostly on which direction you drive: west over the Vimy Memorial Bridge to Barrhaven, north along Riverside Dr to Nepean, or east toward Albion Rd. This guide maps all three directions, with commute times, nearby mosques and weekend Quran and Arabic options.

> - The closest full-time Islamic schools to Riverside South are SNMC Albushra School in Barrhaven (about 7 minutes over the Vimy Memorial Bridge), QAF and Ottawa Islamic School in Nepean (about 15 to 20 minutes), and Ahlul Bayt Islamic School on Albion Rd N (about 17 minutes).

## Islamic school Riverside South families can reach: three directions

## Three communities, three different commutes

### Riverside South

From the Riverside South core around Spratt Rd and Limebank Rd, you have the most choice. The **Vimy Memorial Bridge** and Earl Armstrong Rd put Barrhaven’s Strandherd Dr and Woodroffe Ave about 7 minutes away, which is why many Riverside South families already use SNMC and Barrhaven shops. Heading north, **Limebank Rd to Riverside Dr** brings you to the Hunt Club Rd bridge and then Prince of Wales Dr, which leads straight to Dynes Rd.

### Findlay Creek

Findlay Creek sits further east, near Bank St and Leitrim Rd. Its quickest routes run north on Albion Rd or Bank St, so schools on the east side of the river, such as Ahlul Bayt on Albion Rd N, are closest. To reach QAF, Findlay Creek families typically take Albion Rd and Airport Pkwy to Brookfield Rd, then cross the river at Hog’s Back. Expect about 17 to 22 minutes outside rush hour.

### Manotick

Manotick feels rural, but its commute to Nepean is simple: **Rideau Valley Dr becomes Prince of Wales Dr** and runs north along the river all the way to Dynes Rd. That single road makes QAF one of the easier full-time options for Manotick families, at about 21 to 26 minutes. Barrhaven, about 10 to 15 minutes away, is closer for masjid life and weekend classes.

> **Tip:** If your commute crosses the river, learn both of your bridge options. When one crossing is slow because of construction or a collision, the alternative can save 10 minutes or more.

## Mosques near Riverside South, Findlay Creek and Manotick

These masajid are the ones families in the area most often reach. Check each for current prayer times and children’s classes.

- **SNMC (South Nepean Muslim Community), 3020 Woodroffe Ave, Barrhaven.** About 7 minutes from Riverside South and 10 to 15 minutes from Manotick. A large community centre with two Jumu’ah prayers each Friday, youth events and the SNMC Albushra School on site. See the [SNMC website](https://snmc.ca/).
- **Barrhaven Islamic Centre (MAC), 3971 Greenbank Rd.** About 8 to 12 minutes from Riverside South via Earl Armstrong Rd. It listed Quran halaqa classes for 2026–27 when we checked. Details are on the [Barrhaven Islamic Centre page](https://centres.macnet.ca/bic/).
- **Masjid ar-Rahmah, 1216 Hunt Club Rd, South Keys.** About 10 minutes from Findlay Creek and 13 minutes from Riverside South, and a natural stop for families heading north on the east side of the river.

For families who prefer to keep their children’s Quran class close to home, the masjid you already attend for Jumu’ah is the best place to start asking.

## Weekend Quran and Arabic options near you

Most children in Riverside South and Findlay Creek attend public or Catholic schools during the week. Weekend programs fill the gap.

**Free board Arabic in Findlay Creek.** The OCDSB runs Saturday-morning Arabic from 9:30 to 12:00 at **Vimy Ridge Public School, 4180 Kelly Farm Dr**, from September 12, 2026 to June 19, 2027. It is open to JK to Grade 8 students from any board, with a $20 activity fee for Ontario residents. Details are on the [OCDSB International Languages page](https://www.ocdsb.ca/programs-learning/continuing-education/international-languages-program/international-indigenous-languages-elementary-program).

**Free board Arabic across the bridge.** Riverside South and Manotick families are also close to the OCDSB class at Farley Mowat Public School and the OCSB class at St. Mother Teresa High School, both in Barrhaven. The OCSB class has a $10 materials fee and accepts registrations until March 2027 ([OCSB Continuing Education](https://conted.ocsb.ca/elementary/international-languages-elementary/)).

**QAF Sunday Camp (ages 8–15).** Every Sunday from 11 AM to 3 PM at 900 Dynes Rd. Children study Quran in small groups by level, plus Arabic reading and writing, math and STEM, and soccer, in junior and senior classes. It costs $190 a month from September to June. On Sunday mornings, the drive from Riverside South is usually close to the 15-minute end of the range. More on our [Sunday Quran and Arabic camp in Ottawa](/sunday-camp).

These programs do different jobs. Board classes build Arabic as a language, while Quran programs focus on reading, tajweed and memorization. Our guides to [Arabic classes for kids in Ottawa](/blog/arabic-classes-for-kids-ottawa) and [weekend Islamic schools in Ottawa](/blog/weekend-islamic-schools-ottawa) explain how to combine them.

## Making a longer drive work for a 4-year-old

A 15- to 25-minute drive is very manageable for most kindergarten children, especially with a predictable routine. A few things that help:

1. **Use the car time.** Many families play a short surah or a dua on repeat during the drive. Over a school year, that is a lot of extra Quran listening.
2. **Keep the morning simple.** Lay out clothes and pack lunch and two snacks the night before. QAF is nut-free and all food must be halal, so a small set of go-to lunches saves time. Our [halal lunch ideas for kids](/blog/halal-lunch-ideas-for-kids) can help.
3. **Plan around drop-off.** QAF’s drop-off window is 8:45 to 9:00 AM, and the school day runs from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM, with pick-up from 3:00 to 3:15 PM.
4. **Stay connected.** Parents get daily photos, videos and updates on ClassDojo, so you can talk with your child about their day on the drive home.

## Is QAF right for your Riverside South family?

QAF is a good fit if your child is 4 or 5, you are comfortable with a 15- to 25-minute drive, and you want a full-day Islamic JK and SK with a Montessori-inspired classroom, daily Arabic immersion, Quran from Juz Amma, duas and adab, aligned with Ontario’s kindergarten curriculum. It is especially practical for Manotick families on Prince of Wales Dr and for Riverside South parents who work in central Ottawa. Tuition is $690 a month for 10 months plus a $190 annual resource fee, with a sibling discount.

QAF may not be the best fit if you need extended care, if you prefer a school you can walk to, or if your child needs a program beyond SK. Albushra in Barrhaven or a school on the east side may suit Findlay Creek families who want one school for several years. For children aged 8 to 15, our Sunday Camp may be the easier fit, and it is a good way to see if our [Islamic JK/SK program](/program) suits a younger sibling too.
