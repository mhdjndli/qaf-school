ClassDojo is a free app that lets parents see photos and updates from their child's classroom, check progress and message the teacher privately. To get started, you create a free parent account, then connect to your child's class with the parent code, invite link or QR code your teacher sends you. This ClassDojo parent guide walks you through every step, from setup to notifications, translation, multiple children and privacy.

> **Key takeaways**
> - ClassDojo's core features (Messaging, Portfolios, Class Story and School Story) are free for families. ClassDojo Plus is an optional paid add-on.
> - Your parent code is unique to your child, 8 or 9 digits long, and comes only from your child's teacher.
> - One parent code can be used by up to four parents and guardians, each with a separate, private account.
> - Download the app for push notifications; the website only sends email notifications.
> - Translation into 132 languages is built in: set your translation language, then tap "See translation."

## What is ClassDojo and how do parents use it?

ClassDojo is a communication platform that connects teachers, families and students. For parents, it works like a private, school-only social feed plus a messaging app. You can:

- See **Class Story** posts: photos, videos and announcements from your child's teacher
- See **School Story** posts: school-wide news and events
- View your child's **Portfolio**: classwork the teacher has approved to share with you
- **Message the teacher** privately through Chats
- Get reminders for **class or school events**
- See progress reports, if the teacher shares them

At QAF, parents use ClassDojo for daily photos and videos, progress updates, announcements and messaging teachers. If you're a QAF family, you'll find more on our [parents page](/parents).

## Is ClassDojo free for parents?

Yes. According to ClassDojo's [Plus FAQ](https://help.classdojo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018137732-ClassDojo-Plus-FAQ), all core features (Messaging, Portfolios, Class Story and School Story) are always free for teachers, families and students.

**ClassDojo Plus** is an optional paid subscription for families. Depending on the package, it adds features such as Memories albums to save classroom photos, enhanced progress reports, scheduled and urgent messages with read receipts, calendar sync, and home points. Prices vary, so check the app, and you can try it free for 7 days on iOS or Android. You never need Plus to stay connected with your child's class.

## Step 1: Create your free parent account

You can sign up on the web, iPhone or Android. Here's how on each, based on ClassDojo's current [Create a Parent Account](https://help.classdojo.com/hc/en-us/articles/205417305-Create-a-Parent-Account) instructions.

### On iPhone or iPad

1. Download the ClassDojo app from the App Store.
2. Tap **"I'm a parent."**
3. Enter your email, or use Google or Apple sign-in.
4. Fill in your name and tap **"Create free account."**

### On Android

1. Download the ClassDojo app from the Google Play Store.
2. Tap **"I'm a parent,"** then **"Get Started."**
3. Enter your email, or tap **"Continue with Google."**
4. Add your details, then tap **"Create account."**

### On the web

1. Go to the ClassDojo homepage.
2. Under "Get started as a…", choose **"Parent."**
3. Enter your email and click **"Continue,"** or use Google sign-in.
4. Add your name and details, then click **"Sign up."**

**Tip:** Use an email address you check daily. Even if your teacher invites you by text, you still need an email address to create an account.

## Step 2: Connect to your child's class

Once your account exists, you connect it to your child. ClassDojo offers several ways, and your teacher will tell you which one to use.

### Option A: Parent code (most common)

A parent code is an 8- or 9-digit code unique to your child, which you receive from your child's teacher.

**On iPhone or Android:**

1. Log into your parent account in the app.
2. Tap the **"Kids"** tab at the bottom of the screen.
3. Tap the purple **"Add code"** button.
4. Enter the code and tap **"Submit."**

**On the web:**

1. Log into your parent account.
2. On the left side of the screen, click the purple **"+ Add a child."**
3. Click **"Add child using code."**
4. Enter the code and click **"Verify code."**

### Option B: Email or text invite

If your teacher sent you an email or text invitation, tap the link. You'll be taken to a screen to sign up or log in, and once logged in you'll be connected to your child's class.

### Option C: Class link, school link or QR code

If your teacher shared a class or school link, open it and follow the prompts. If they shared a class QR code, scan it with your phone's camera. Your connection becomes active once the teacher accepts your request.

### Option D: Search for the teacher

No code or link yet? In the app, tap **"Find your teacher"** (or on the web, **"Add child through a teacher"**), search for your child's school and teacher, and send a request. It will show as pending until the teacher accepts it, after which you'll see a "New Class Connection" post in your Story feed.

**If your code doesn't work:** Double-check every digit, make sure you're logged into your parent account (not a student account), and ask the teacher to confirm the code. ClassDojo's step-by-step guide to [connecting to your child's class](https://help.classdojo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050894111-How-Do-I-Connect-to-My-Child-s-Class) covers every method.

## Step 3: Add the other parent or guardian

Each parent code can be used by **up to four parents and guardians**. The second parent simply creates their own account and enters the same code.

Every parent account linked to a code is separate and private. All connected parents can see the Class Story, Portfolio and reports, but each person's private messages with the teacher stay private to that person. If grandparents help with pick-up, ask the teacher whether they should be connected too.

## Step 4: Understand the Story feed

The **Story** tab is your main feed. It shows posts from every class and school you're connected to, in one place.

| Feed | Who posts | What you'll see |
|---|---|---|
| Class Story | Your child's teacher | Classroom photos, videos, learning highlights, reminders |
| School Story | School leaders | School-wide announcements, events, closures |
| Portfolio | Your child, approved by the teacher | Your child's own work, drawings, videos |

You can like posts, and comment if the teacher has turned comments on. Class and School Story posts may include photos of several children, so please treat them as private to the class community and don't share them on social media.

**Tip for families with several children:** On iOS, you can filter your Story feed by child. Tap **"All Stories"** at the top of the Story tab and choose a name. ClassDojo says this filter is currently iOS only.

## Step 5: View your child's Portfolio

The Portfolio is your child's personal digital folder of work. It might include a photo of a drawing, a video of your child reading, or a completed activity.

Teachers must approve Portfolio posts before families can see them. Once approved, posts are visible only to your child, the family members connected to your child, and the teachers connected to the class. In the early years, Portfolio posts are a wonderful prompt for conversation: "Tell me about this picture. What did you build?"

## Step 6: Message your child's teacher

To send a message:

1. Open the app or website and go to the **Chats** tab.
2. Tap your child's teacher's name.
3. Type in the compose box at the bottom and tap **"Send."**

Teachers can also create group chats with several parents. Messages in a group chat are visible to everyone in that thread.

### Messaging etiquette that teachers appreciate

- **Keep it brief and specific.** "Ahmed has a dentist appointment Thursday at 1:30 PM; I'll pick him up at 1:15" is perfect.
- **Respect teaching time.** Teachers usually reply after class, not instantly.
- **Respect Quiet Hours.** Teachers can set Quiet Hours, during which they don't receive message notifications. Your message will still arrive; they'll simply see it later.
- **Use the phone or office for emergencies.** For urgent same-day matters, such as a pick-up change, follow your school's office procedures rather than relying on an app message.
- **Bring big concerns to a meeting.** Use a message to request a time to talk.

You can also delete a message you sent by mistake, and download your message history from the website.

## Step 7: Set up notifications

Getting notifications right is the difference between seeing today's class photos and missing them. According to ClassDojo's [notifications guide](https://help.classdojo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050778411-Notifications-and-Your-Parent-Account), you'll be notified when:

- The teacher sends you a private message
- There's a new Class Story or School Story post
- The teacher approves a new Portfolio post
- A teacher schedules reminders for class or school events

Important details:

1. **App users get push notifications; web-only users get emails.** It isn't possible to receive both.
2. **Turn on push notifications in your phone's settings,** not inside the ClassDojo app. On iPhone, go to Settings, then Notifications, then ClassDojo. On Android, go to Settings, then Apps, then ClassDojo, then Notifications.
3. **Not getting notifications?** Make sure you're logged in, the app is up to date, and notifications are fully enabled. Web users should check spam or junk folders.

## Step 8: Turn on translation

ClassDojo has built-in translation, which is a big help for families where English isn't the first language at home.

**On the web:** Click your name in the upper right, select **"Account Settings,"** choose your **"Translation language preference"** (132 languages are available) and click **"Save changes."**

**On iPhone or Android:** Tap your profile icon in the upper left, tap the gear icon beside your account, then choose your **Content** (iOS) or **Translation Language** (Android) setting.

Then, as you scroll through Story posts or messages, tap **"See translation"** below a caption or message. Your translation language setting is also used as the language of messages you send, so set it carefully.

## Step 9: Add more than one child

You only need one parent account, even with several children in different classes or schools. In the app or on the web, tap **"+ Add a child"** under your connected children and use a new parent code, link or teacher search for each child. Always log into your existing account rather than creating a new one; this keeps all your children in one place.

New school year? Your account carries over. Simply connect to the new teacher, and you can remove previous classes from your account settings.

## ClassDojo privacy: what parents should know

Parents often ask who can see their child's information. Based on ClassDojo's help centre and [privacy policy](https://www.classdojo.com/privacy/), which includes a section on Canadian data privacy:

- **No student's account or Portfolio is visible to the general public** through ClassDojo.
- **Portfolio posts** are visible only to your child, connected family members and the class teachers, after teacher approval.
- **Class and School Story posts** can include photos of several students and are visible to connected families in that class or school.
- **Your private messages** with the teacher are visible to you and the teacher, not to other parents.
- **Parent Chat** (where available) lets families message each other without sharing phone numbers or emails. It's optional: in **Account Settings, then Chats,** you can choose whether anyone on ClassDojo, families in your child's classes and school, or only teachers and staff can start chats with you. You can block or report people at any time.

If you have specific privacy questions, ask your school what it shares on ClassDojo and how it handles photo permissions.

## Make the most of ClassDojo as a parent

ClassDojo is most valuable when it sparks conversation at home. A few habits we see working well for families:

- **Use photos as conversation starters.** Instead of "How was school?", try "I saw you planting seeds today. What did you plant?"
- **Celebrate effort, not just results.** Notice the work behind a Portfolio post.
- **Check once a day, not constantly.** A quick look in the evening is enough.
- **Keep your notification settings tidy** so important messages stand out.
