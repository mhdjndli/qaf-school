If your child is starting Junior Kindergarten in September 2027, the best time to begin looking at Islamic schools is this fall, and the most important decisions usually land between January and April. This Islamic school admissions timeline lays out what to do each month, from shortlisting schools in October 2026 to the first day in September 2027, so you never feel rushed into a choice.

> **Key takeaways**
> - Children born in **2023** start JK in September 2027; children born in **2022** start SK.
> - Use **October to December 2026** to research, shortlist and book visits. Private and Islamic schools admit on their own timelines, and popular classes can fill early.
> - Public board kindergarten registration typically opens around **December or January**. Even if you choose an Islamic school, it is wise to know your public option.
> - Aim to **decide and confirm a spot by spring 2027**, then use the summer for routines and readiness.
> - Keep a single folder of documents (proof of age, address, immunizations) ready from the start.

## Who is starting school in September 2027?

In Ontario, children start JK in September of the calendar year they turn four. The [OCDSB kindergarten registration page](https://www.ocdsb.ca/our-schools/registration/kindergarten) puts it simply: if your child turns four by December 31, they can start that September.

For September 2027:

| Child's birth year | Grade in September 2027 | Typical age on the first day |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | Junior Kindergarten (JK) | 3 years 8 months to 4 years 8 months |
| 2022 | Senior Kindergarten (SK) | 4 years 8 months to 5 years 8 months |
| 2021 | Grade 1 | 5 years 8 months to 6 years 8 months |

Kindergarten is optional in Ontario because [compulsory school attendance begins at age six](https://www.ontario.ca/document/ontario-schools-kindergarten-grade-12-policy-and-program-requirements-2024/student-records). That gives you flexibility, but most families still start in JK. If your child has a late-year birthday, read our guide to [December birthdays and JK](/blog/december-birthday-jk-ontario) before deciding.

## Why Islamic school admissions run on a different clock

Public boards accept every eligible child in their area, so timing mostly affects planning. Private and Islamic schools are different. Each school sets its own admissions process, class sizes and fees, and classes have a fixed number of spots.

Many independent schools also give early consideration to returning families and siblings before opening remaining spots. Practices vary, so ask each school how it handles priority and waitlists.

That is why this timeline starts a full school year ahead. Our general guide to [junior kindergarten in Ottawa](/blog/junior-kindergarten-ottawa-guide) covers the public registration process in detail; this post focuses on the Islamic and private school path, with public dates included as a reference point.

## Islamic school admissions timeline: month by month

### October to November 2026: research and shortlist

This is the calmest time to start. Schools are settled into the year, so visits show you a real working classroom rather than the first-week bustle.

**To do:**

1. List what matters most: daily Quran and Arabic, Islamic environment, full-day schedule, distance, budget, approach (Montessori-inspired, play-based, academic).
2. Read through the [Islamic schools in Ottawa](/blog/islamic-schools-in-ottawa-guide) and narrow to two to four schools.
3. Map the commute at drop-off time from your home and workplace.
4. Submit inquiries to your shortlisted schools and ask for a visit.

### December 2026: budget and paperwork

December is short and busy, with winter break, so use it for planning rather than visits.

**To do:**

1. Build a realistic budget for tuition plus extras. Our guide to [Ottawa Islamic school fees](/blog/ottawa-islamic-school-fees-explained) shows what usually is and isn't included.
2. Find out which costs may count as child care expenses at tax time, and keep every receipt.
3. Start a document folder: birth certificate or passport, proof of address, immunization record, custody papers if relevant.
4. Watch for public board kindergarten registration. For September 2026 entry, the OCDSB announced in December 2025 that kindergarten registration was open.

### January 2027: visits and information nights

January is peak season for school visits and public kindergarten information nights.

**To do:**

1. Visit your shortlisted Islamic schools, ideally on a regular school morning. Our guide to [what to expect at a school visit](/blog/visit-islamic-school-what-to-expect) explains how visits usually run.
2. Bring a list. Our [40 questions to ask on a school tour](/blog/questions-to-ask-islamic-school-tour) cover program, staff, safety, communication and fees.
3. Attend a public school information night if you want a fair comparison.
4. Ask each school directly: how many spots are open for JK, when do you confirm offers, and what deposit or registration step secures a place?

### February to March 2027: decide and apply

By now you have seen the schools and compared costs. Many families make the decision in this window.

**To do:**

1. Score each school against your list. Our guide on [how to choose an Islamic kindergarten](/blog/choosing-islamic-kindergarten-checklist) offers a simple scorecard.
2. Complete the application or registration for your first choice.
3. Read the enrolment agreement carefully, including fees, payment schedule and refund terms. Ontario's [guidance on private schools](https://www.ontario.ca/page/private-schools) suggests asking whether the school has written contracts covering fees and refunds.
4. If you are holding a public spot as a backup, keep that registration current until your private place is confirmed.

Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin in early February, depending on moon sighting. Many schools adjust schedules during Ramadan, so confirm visit times in advance.

### April to May 2027: confirm and plan logistics

**To do:**

1. Confirm your child's place in writing and note any payment dates.
2. If you registered with a public board as a backup and no longer need it, let them know so the spot can go to another family.
3. Arrange before- or after-school care if your work hours require it.
4. Book a routine check-up and make sure immunizations are up to date before school starts.
5. Ask whether the school offers a spring orientation or a second visit for your child.

### June 2027: orientation and supply lists

**To do:**

1. Attend any welcome or orientation event.
2. Collect the supply list and label everything, including lunch containers. Check the school's nut-free and food policies before you shop.
3. Set up any parent communication app the school uses, so you are ready for September announcements.

### July to August 2027: readiness and routines

Summer is for gentle preparation, not workbooks.

**To do:**

1. Shift bedtime and wake-up time toward the school schedule two to three weeks before the first day.
2. Practise self-care skills: toileting, jacket zips, opening lunch containers. Our [JK readiness checklist](/blog/jk-readiness-checklist) has the full list.
3. Introduce a few short surahs and duas at home if your child is new to them, so the classroom feels familiar.
4. Do a practice drive at drop-off time and point out the school.

### September 2027: the first weeks

Keep goodbyes short and warm, keep evenings calm, and expect tiredness. Our [first week of JK tips](/blog/first-week-of-jk-tips) walk through the first days in detail.

## Starting late? What to do if you are behind

If you are reading this in spring or summer 2027, you have not missed your chance. Here is a compressed version:

1. **Call your top two schools this week** and ask about remaining spots and waitlists.
2. **Visit within two weeks.** Most schools can arrange a visit quickly.
3. **Register with your public board in parallel**, since public schools accept eligible children year-round. The [OCSB registration page](https://www.ocsb.ca/our-schools/register/) and OCDSB pages both take online registrations.
4. **Decide quickly but carefully.** Read the enrolment agreement before paying anything.

If no Islamic kindergarten spot is available, a public JK combined with a weekend program is a solid plan. See our guide to [weekend Islamic schools in Ottawa](/blog/weekend-islamic-schools-ottawa).

## Documents to have ready

Requirements differ by school, but most ask for:

- **Proof of age:** birth certificate, passport or permanent resident card
- **Proof of address:** for public boards, typically two documents (the OCDSB, for example, asks for one housing document plus one utility, bank or insurance document)
- **Immunization record:** keep your child's vaccination record up to date and handy
- **Custody or court documents**, where relevant
- **Health information:** allergies, medical conditions, medications
- **Previous reports** from child care or preschool, if you have them

Newcomer families and families with temporary immigration status may need extra steps for public registration, so contact the board's admissions office early.
