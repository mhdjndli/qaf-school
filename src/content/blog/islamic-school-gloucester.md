Looking for an Islamic school in Gloucester? The area covers a lot of ground, so the answer to "where is the nearest Islamic school?" depends on which part you live in. For families in Blossom Park, Greenboro, Hunt Club Park and Elmvale Acres, the closest full-day Islamic kindergarten is QAF School at 900 Dynes Rd, about 8–11 km away. That is roughly 12–20 minutes by car outside rush hour. Weekend Quran and Arabic are much closer, with a Saturday program at Blossom Park Public School and Quran schools at Masjid ar-Rahmah on Hunt Club Rd.

Below, we break Gloucester into its main pockets and show the real drive, the nearest weekend options, and how families here usually combine school and Islamic learning.

> **Key takeaways**
> - We could not verify a full-time Islamic school inside Blossom Park, Greenboro or Elmvale as of October 2026.
> - QAF's Islamic JK/SK in Carleton Heights is about 12–20 minutes from most of Gloucester's south-central neighbourhoods.
> - Iqraa Southkeys at Blossom Park Public School runs Saturday Arabic plus Quran and Islamic Studies for JK to Grade 8.
> - Masjid ar-Rahmah (1216 Hunt Club Rd) is the closest masjid with structured children's Quran classes for Greenboro and Blossom Park.
> - Elmvale Acres is the exception: its drive heads west on Riverside Dr, not along Hunt Club Rd.

## Blossom Park and Greenboro: the Hunt Club corridor

Most families in Blossom Park and Greenboro head west on Hunt Club Rd. They pick up the Airport Parkway north, then cross the Rideau at Hog's Back to reach Prince of Wales Dr and Dynes Rd. It is a straightforward drive with no highway merges, which matters when there is a sleepy four-year-old in the back seat.

This corridor also has the most weekend choice in Gloucester:

- **Saturdays at Blossom Park Public School.** The Iqraa Southkeys program pairs an OCDSB International Languages Arabic class (9:30 AM to noon, JK to Grade 8) with an Islamic Studies and Quran memorization block run by the Assunnah Muslims Association from noon to 2:30 PM. The AMA lists the second block at $300 for the year.
- **Masjid ar-Rahmah, 1216 Hunt Club Rd.** Its Albayan Quran schools run on Tuesday and Thursday evenings and on Sunday mornings for children from age 6. Current details are on the [masjid's school page](https://www.mymasjid.ca/school/).

Transit is better here than in much of the suburbs. The O-Train Line 2 has stations at Greenboro and South Keys, according to [OC Transpo](https://www.octranspo.com/en/our-services/stations1/greenboro1/). That helps older children reach Saturday programs. For a kindergartener's daily trip across the river, though, most families drive.

## Elmvale Acres: a different direction

Elmvale Acres sits farther north, near St. Laurent Blvd and Smyth Rd. From here the quickest way to Dynes Rd is west along Pleasant Park Rd to Riverside Dr, then over the river at Hog's Back, about 9 km in total. You skip Hunt Club Rd entirely.

This route suits parents who work around the Smyth Rd health campus (CHEO and The Ottawa Hospital's General Campus). Drop-off at 8:45 AM in Carleton Heights, then back along Riverside Dr, can make a loop rather than a long detour. Test it once in real morning traffic before deciding.

For weekend Quran, Masjid ar-Rahmah is about a 10-minute drive south. You will also find Quran programs across the city in our guide to [Quran programs for kids in Ottawa](/blog/quran-classes-for-kids-ottawa).

## Your Gloucester board school vs a full-day Islamic kindergarten

Every Gloucester address has a designated school in each of Ottawa's four boards. Look yours up with the [OCDSB school locator](https://www.ocdsb.ca/our_schools/school_locator) and the [OCSB school locator](https://www.ocsb.ca/schools/) before you compare anything else. The two French-language boards, CEPEO and CECCE, are options if you are eligible.

Here is how the two weekday paths compare for a Gloucester four-year-old:

| | Board kindergarten | QAF Islamic JK/SK |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free | $690/month × 10 + $190 resource fee ($7,090/year) |
| Distance | Usually walkable or bused | 12–20 minutes by car |
| Language | About 50% English, 50% French (English boards) | English with daily Arabic immersion |
| Faith | Accommodation on request | Quran, duas, adab and Islamic Studies every day |
| Curriculum | Ontario kindergarten curriculum | Aligned with Ontario's kindergarten curriculum, Montessori-inspired |
| Food | Varies by family | Halal and nut-free; families pack lunch and two snacks |
| Hours | Board bell times | 9:00 AM–3:00 PM, drop-off 8:45–9:00 |

Neither path is wrong. Board kindergarten plus a Saturday program at Blossom Park PS is a strong, low-cost combination for many Gloucester families. A full-day Islamic kindergarten makes more sense when you want Arabic and Quran built into every day during the years children absorb language fastest. We explain that reasoning in [why early Arabic immersion matters](/blog/why-early-arabic-immersion-matters).

## What a Gloucester family's week can look like

Here is one example plan for a family with a five-year-old and a ten-year-old living in Greenboro:

1. **Monday to Friday:** the five-year-old attends [our Islamic kindergarten in Ottawa](/program), 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Daily photos and teacher messages come through ClassDojo. The ten-year-old attends the local board school.
2. **Tuesday and Thursday evening:** the ten-year-old does Quran at Masjid ar-Rahmah, about five minutes from home.
3. **Saturday:** free for family time, groceries and rest.
4. **Sunday (alternative):** if the older child needs more Arabic, STEM and sports, [QAF Sunday Camp](/sunday-camp) runs Sundays 11 AM to 3 PM at 900 Dynes Rd for ages 8 to 15, at $190 a month.

The point is balance. One main Quran program per child is usually enough. A second one only works if your child is genuinely keen and still sleeping well.

Before you lock in a plan, ask three questions:

- **Who drives, and when?** Write down the morning and afternoon driver for each day, including the Saturday or Sunday program.
- **What happens in winter?** Hunt Club Rd and Riverside Dr are slower after snowfall, so build in a buffer from December to March.
- **Where does revision happen?** Weekend memorization only sticks with a few minutes of review at home, so choose a program whose homework you can realistically support.

## Carpooling and logistics across Gloucester

The Dynes Rd drive is short enough that many families share it. A few practical tips:

- **Pair up by corridor.** Blossom Park and Greenboro families share the Hunt Club route. Elmvale families share Riverside Dr.
- **Use the pick-up window.** Pick-up runs 3:00 to 3:15 PM, so one carpool parent can collect two families' children in one trip.
- **Label everything.** Lunch bags, boots and mitts travel between cars all winter.
- **Agree on a halal, nut-free snack rule** for the car too, so no one is caught out.

## Is QAF right for your family?

QAF suits Gloucester families with a child aged 4 or 5 who want a full school day where Quran, Arabic and Islamic manners are part of the routine. It also suits parents who can manage a 12–20 minute drive each way.

It is not the right fit if you need a school within walking distance, need board busing, or want one school from kindergarten through Grade 8. We only offer JK and SK. If your children are 6 or 7, look at Masjid ar-Rahmah's Albayan classes or the Saturday program at Blossom Park PS, since QAF Sunday Camp starts at age 8. And if you live in the eastern parts of Gloucester, such as Blackburn Hamlet or Beacon Hill, compare the drive carefully; you may be closer to east-end options in [weekend Islamic schools in Ottawa](/blog/weekend-islamic-schools-ottawa).
