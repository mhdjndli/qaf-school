If you are searching for an Islamic school in Kanata, here is the honest answer: as of October 2026, we could not find a full-time Islamic school inside Kanata itself. Kanata families usually choose a full-time Islamic school in nearby Stittsville or Bells Corners, or a public school during the week plus one of Kanata’s strong weekend Islamic schools. This Islamic school Kanata guide covers all of those options, and it is upfront about when a drive to our school in Nepean makes sense and when it does not.

> **Key takeaways**
> - The closest full-time Islamic schools to Kanata are Marifah School and Fitra School on Stittsville Main St, and Tarbiyah Learning Academy in Bells Corners, each roughly 8 to 15 minutes from most of Kanata.
> - Kanata’s weekend programs are its strength: the Kanata Muslim Association’s Hikmah Saturday School, Alnour School’s Kanata campus and free OCDSB Arabic at Roch Carrier and Jack Donohue public schools.
> - QAF School on Dynes Rd is 20 to 30 km from Kanata and Stittsville. Outside rush hour that is about 20 to 30 minutes, but weekday mornings on Highway 417 can take much longer.
> - For most Kanata families, QAF Sunday Camp (ages 8–15) is a more realistic fit than a daily kindergarten commute.

## Stittsville: the closest full-time hub

Stittsville Main St has become a real centre for Muslim families in the west end, which is good news for Kanata parents, especially in Bridlewood, Glen Cairn and Kanata South.

**Marifah School** runs the widest range of programs in the area, according to its website: toddler and preschool child care, a full-time JK to Grade 8 school with French, Arabic, Islamic Studies and Quran, a separate French Immersion stream for JK and SK, and a high school learning centre where students take online credit courses in an Islamic setting. It also runs a Quran Maktab and a Sunday Islamic School for ages 5 to 12. Details are on the [Marifah School website](https://marifah.ca/).

**Fitra School** is a second full-time option on Stittsville Main St. It lists Quran and Arabic among its subjects.

The **Stittsville Muslim Association** at 1469 Stittsville Main St also lists an SMA Sunday School, the Sana Saturday School, kids camps and a youth leadership program on the [Stittsville Muslim Association website](https://www.stittsvillemuslims.ca).

## Bells Corners and the west end

For families in Kanata North and Kanata Lakes, Highway 417 and Moodie Dr put Bells Corners about 10 minutes away. **Tarbiyah Learning Academy** is on the second floor of the Jami Omar mosque building at 3990 Old Richmond Rd. It lists an early childhood program, a full kindergarten program, elementary and junior high, and students use the masjid space for Quran lessons. From Bridlewood, it is often the very closest full-time Islamic school, at about 6 km.

**Al-Furqan Ottawa**, a full-time school run under the Muslim Association of Canada, is another west-end option; its listings describe a hifz-focused program. Ask the school about current grades and admission.

## Kanata’s weekend Islamic schools

Many Kanata families keep their children in public school during the week and invest in a strong weekend program. Kanata is well served here.

- **Hikmah Saturday School (Kanata Muslim Association), 351 Sandhill Rd.** For JK to Grade 8, with Quran, Islamic Studies in English, and Arabic and Urdu, plus congregational Zuhr prayer and outings. It has run since September 2012.
- **Al-Madinah Quran School (KMA).** A Quran tajweed and memorization program for ages 5 to 14. See the [KMA education page](https://www.kanatamuslims.ca/education) and ask the association for current days and times.
- **Alnour School, Kanata campus, 101 Penrith St.** A Saturday school teaching Arabic language arts, Islamic Studies and Quran, all in Arabic. Registration for 2026–27 is open on the Alnour website.
- **Free OCDSB Arabic classes.** Saturday mornings from 9:30 to 12:00 at Roch Carrier Elementary School (401 Stonehaven Dr), Jack Donohue Public School (101 Penrith St) and, in Stittsville, Westwind Public School (111 Hartsmere Dr). Open to JK to Grade 8 students from any board, with a $20 activity fee for Ontario residents ([OCDSB International Languages](https://www.ocdsb.ca/programs-learning/continuing-education/international-languages-program/international-indigenous-languages-elementary-program)).
- **Marifah Sunday Islamic School (ages 5–12) and SMA weekend classes** in Stittsville, covered above.

For a city-wide list, see our guide to [weekend Islamic schools in Ottawa](/blog/weekend-islamic-schools-ottawa).

## The honest commute math: Kanata to Dynes Rd

QAF is at 900 Dynes Rd, just off Prince of Wales Dr in Nepean. Here is how far that is from Kanata and Stittsville.

| Starting point | Approx. distance | Typical drive outside rush hour | Main route |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kanata Centrum area | 20 km | About 20–25 minutes | Highway 417, Maitland Ave, Baseline Rd |
| Kanata Lakes, Kanata North | 22 km | About 25–30 minutes | Highway 417, Maitland Ave, Baseline Rd |
| Glen Cairn, Bridlewood | 21 km | About 23–28 minutes | Old Richmond Rd and West Hunt Club Rd, or Highway 417 |
| Stittsville | 29.5 km | About 30–35 minutes | Carp Rd to Highway 417, then Baseline Rd |

From the highway, the last stretch is south on Prince of Wales Dr to Dynes Rd. QAF’s drop-off window is 8:45 to 9:00 AM, which puts you on Highway 417 eastbound in the morning peak, when traffic is often heavy. Real drop-off times can be much longer than the figures above, so test the drive on a school day before you decide.

**When the drive can make sense:**

- One parent already works near the Experimental Farm, Carleton University, Merivale or Hunt Club, so QAF is on the way.
- You specifically want a Montessori-inspired, full-day [Islamic JK/SK program](/program) with daily Arabic immersion, and you are willing to trade time for that fit.
- You are planning a move toward Nepean or Barrhaven within a year or two.

**When it probably does not:** if both parents work in Kanata, if you have a younger child at home with nap times to protect, or if the commute would push you to leave before 8:00 AM. In those cases, a school in Stittsville or Bells Corners, or public kindergarten plus a weekend program, will likely serve your family better. We would rather tell you that now than have a child spend two hours a day in the car.

## Sunday Camp: the easier fit for Kanata families

On Sundays, the picture changes. QAF Sunday Camp runs from 11 AM to 3 PM, when Highway 417 is usually much quieter, so the drive from Kanata is typically close to the 20- to 30-minute range.

Sunday Camp is for ages 8 to 15, in junior and senior classes. Children study Quran in small groups by level, plus Arabic reading and writing, math and STEM, and soccer. Tuition is $190 a month from September to June. It suits Kanata children who already go to public school and do Saturday activities, and it complements a Saturday program like Hikmah or Alnour rather than replacing it. See our [Sunday Quran and Arabic camp in Ottawa](/sunday-camp) for details, and our guide to [Quran classes in Ottawa](/blog/quran-classes-for-kids-ottawa) to compare approaches.

## Mosques, libraries and family life in Kanata and Stittsville

**Masajid:** The Kanata Muslim Association at 351 Sandhill Rd in Kanata North and the Stittsville Muslim Association at 1469 Stittsville Main St are the two main community centres, and both list children’s and youth programs. Jami Omar in Bells Corners is close for Kanata South.

**Libraries:** The Ottawa Public Library has three branches in the area: Beaverbrook (Campeau Dr, Kanata North), Hazeldean (Castlefrank Rd, Kanata South) and Stittsville (Stittsville Main St). Each offers free children’s programs.

**Public and Catholic schools:** All four Ottawa boards serve Kanata and Stittsville, and kindergarten is free and full-day. Use the [OCDSB school locator](https://www.ocdsb.ca/our-schools/school-locator) to find your designated school, and see our [Ottawa school boards comparison](/blog/ottawa-school-boards-compared) to understand your choices.

## Is QAF right for your Kanata family?

For weekday kindergarten, QAF is a good fit for a smaller group of Kanata families: those with a 4- or 5-year-old whose daily route already passes through Nepean, or who strongly prefer our approach and accept the drive. Our full-day JK and SK runs from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday to Friday, September to June, and is Montessori-inspired and aligned with Ontario’s kindergarten curriculum, with daily Arabic, Quran from Juz Amma, duas and adab. Tuition is $690 a month for 10 months plus a $190 annual resource fee.

For most Kanata families, a closer full-time school in Stittsville or Bells Corners, or a weekend school in Kanata, will be the more practical daily choice. For children aged 8 to 15, Sunday Camp is often the easiest way to be part of QAF from the west end.
