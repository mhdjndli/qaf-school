The best winter break activities in Ottawa for December 2026 mix free outdoor fun with a few indoor anchors for very cold days. Think tobogganing on city-maintained hills, skating at outdoor rinks, Greenbelt trails, museum free hours and library programs. Ottawa's school boards are expected to close from Monday, December 21, 2026 to Friday, January 1, 2027, so most families have two full weeks to fill. This guide gives you a day-by-day plan, a budget breakdown and tips for Muslim families on short winter prayer times.

> **Key takeaways**
> - The Ottawa Catholic School Board's calendar shows winter break from December 21, 2026 to January 1, 2027, with classes resuming January 4. Check your own board's calendar.
> - The best free options are city tobogganing hills, outdoor rinks, NCC winter trails, and museum free hours: Ingenium museums from 4 to 5 PM daily, and the Museum of Nature on Thursdays from 5 to 8 PM.
> - The Rideau Canal Skateway usually opens in January, not December. Don't build your break around it.
> - Many attractions close on December 25 and change hours around New Year's Day. Confirm before you drive.
> - Days are short. Dhuhr, Asr and Maghrib can all fall within one afternoon outing, so pack a travel prayer mat.

## Free outdoor fun

### 1. Tobogganing on city hills

**What it is:** Ottawa has dozens of approved toboggan hills, all maintained by the City of Ottawa. Ottawa Tourism counts 75. Well-known family hills include Bruce Pit and Craig Henry Park in Nepean, Carlington Park, Conroy Pit near Hunt Club, Green's Creek and Loyola Park in Gloucester, Walter Baker Park in Kanata and Westboro Kiwanis Park.
**Best for:** Every age, from toddlers on gentle slopes to tweens chasing speed.
**Our take:** Conroy Pit is lit at night according to the [NCC's tobogganing page](https://ncc-ccn.gc.ca/places/tobogganing-greenbelt), which helps when the sun sets around 4:20 PM. Bruce Pit is not lit. Helmets are strongly recommended, snowboarding and jumping off snow bumps are prohibited, and closed hills are signed.

### 2. Outdoor skating rinks

**What it is:** The City runs outdoor rinks across Ottawa, which you can find on its interactive rink map. The Sens Rink of Dreams at City Hall, on Laurier Avenue West, is a free downtown option.
**Best for:** Families who want a short skate close to home.
**Our take:** Natural rinks depend on cold weather, and early-winter ice can be patchy. Check the rink's status online before loading everyone into the car.

### 3. Rideau Hall Rink

**What it is:** A refrigerated outdoor rink on the grounds of the Governor General's residence, with a winter pavilion in the restored Dairy Building, according to the [NCC's winter ideas list](https://ncc-ccn.gc.ca/blog/35-ideas-winter-activities-ottawa-gatineau).
**Best for:** A special-feeling skate with younger children.
**Our take:** Refrigerated ice is a big advantage in December, when natural rinks may not be ready yet. Check the current season's hours and access details before you go.

### 4. Greenbelt trails and snowshoeing

**What it is:** The NCC's Greenbelt has winter trails at Mer Bleue Bog, the Old Quarry Trail in Stony Swamp, Shirleys Bay and Green's Creek, which has a 5.5-km winter trail. Mādahòkì Farm in the Greenbelt also has a farm store and a snowshoe trail, and kids can meet Ojibwe Spirit Horses there.
**Best for:** Families with kids aged 4 and up who can walk 2–5 km.
**Our take:** The Mer Bleue boardwalk is short, flat and beautiful in snow. It's ideal for a first family snowshoe.

### 5. River winter trails

**What it is:** The Rideau Winter Trail runs on the frozen river and can be reached from Hurdman Station on the O-Train. The Kichi Sibi Winter Trail runs along the Ottawa River near Westboro Beach.
**Best for:** Older kids on skis, or families with a sled for little ones.
**Our take:** Trail conditions depend on snowfall and ice, so check each trail's update before you go.

### 6. What about the Rideau Canal Skateway?

The [Rideau Canal Skateway](https://ncc-ccn.gc.ca/places/rideau-canal-skateway) is 7.8 km long and free when open. The NCC says the season typically runs from January to early March, depending on the weather. Treat it as a bonus if it opens early, not as your winter break plan. It will be the centrepiece of Winterlude, which runs from January 29 to February 15, 2027.

## Indoor options for very cold days

### 7. Canada Science and Technology Museum (and the other Ingenium museums)

**What it is:** Hands-on science at 1867 St. Laurent Blvd. It is open Tuesday to Sunday from 9 AM to 5 PM, and open on statutory holidays except December 25 ([Ingenium visitor info](https://ingenium.ca/scitech/en/visit)).
**Price:** Paid, with free admission daily from 4 to 5 PM.
**Our take:** The free hour is perfect after an afternoon of tobogganing. The Canada Aviation and Space Museum and the Canada Agriculture and Food Museum also offer free admission from 4 to 5 PM. Our guide to [things to do with kids in Ottawa](/blog/things-to-do-with-kids-ottawa) covers all four national museums in more detail.

### 8. Canadian Museum of Nature

**What it is:** Dinosaurs, mammals, minerals and live insects at 240 McLeod Street.
**Price:** $19 for children 3–12 during the day, free for ages 0–2, and free general admission on Thursdays from 5 to 8 PM ([admission fees](https://nature.ca/en/visit-us/plan-your-visit/admission-fees/)).
**Our take:** The Thursday evening free window is a great outing during the break, as long as you check holiday hours first.

### 9. Canadian Museum of History and Canadian Children's Museum

**What it is:** A national museum at 100 Laurier Street in Gatineau, with the Canadian Children's Museum inside, a favourite for ages 2–10.
**Price:** $18 for children 3–12, a $68 family ticket and free admission on Thursdays from 5 to 7 PM. Booking online saves 10% ([plan your visit](https://www.historymuseum.ca/plan-your-visit)).
**Our take:** Busy days are first-come, first-served at the Children's Museum, so arrive early on weekdays.

### 10. Ottawa Public Library

**What it is:** Free storytimes, crafts, STEM drop-ins and holiday programs at branches across the city. You can also borrow museum Access Passes with a library card.
**Best for:** Budget days and kids who need a calm indoor reset.
**Our take:** Pick up a stack of books for a reading challenge, then reward finished books with a family outing.

### 11. City of Ottawa holiday camps, swims and skates

**What it is:** The City posts holiday public swim and skate schedules and, in many years, short holiday day camps. All are bookable through its recreation portal, [register.ottawa.ca](https://register.ottawa.ca/).
**Best for:** Working parents who need care on a few weekdays.
**Our take:** Popular spots fill quickly once winter registration opens in the fall, so set a reminder.

## A sample two-week winter break plan

Use this as a template and swap days around the weather. Mornings work best for outdoor play, when it's brightest. Plan indoor stops for after Asr.

| Day | Plan |
|---|---|
| Mon, Dec 21 | Sleep in, library visit, pick out books for a reading challenge |
| Tue, Dec 22 | Tobogganing in the morning; Science and Technology Museum free hour (4–5 PM) |
| Wed, Dec 23 | Mer Bleue boardwalk walk; hot chocolate and Quran revision at home |
| Thu, Dec 24 | Baking day; Museum of Nature free evening (5–8 PM), if open, so check holiday hours |

| Sat, Dec 26 | Outdoor rink skate; sadaqah outing (donate food or winter clothes) |
| Sun, Dec 27 | Weekend Islamic class if it's running, or a family halaqa |
| Mon, Dec 28 | Snow fort and snow science experiments in the backyard |
| Tue, Dec 29 | Rideau Hall Rink or a river winter trail |

Build in two or three "nothing" days. Children need rest, and a slow day with board games is a gift in its own right. Our [screen-free weekend ideas](/blog/screen-free-weekend-ideas) list has more low-cost things to do at home.

## Keeping faith in the routine over the break

Two weeks without school is a great chance to build Islamic habits that don't fit into busy school weeks.

- **Short days, three prayers in one afternoon.** In late December, the sun sets in Ottawa around 4:20 PM, so Dhuhr, Asr and Maghrib land close together. Check a reliable local timetable, pack a travel mat, and find a quiet corner at museums.
- **A winter Quran goal.** Choose one short surah to memorize or revise over the break, and celebrate with an outing when it's done.
- **Kindness in the cold.** Winter is a natural time for sadaqah: donate warm clothing, help a neighbour shovel or bake for someone who lives alone. See our [sadaqah ideas for kids](/blog/charity-activities-for-kids).
- **The December conversation.** If your children have questions about Christmas around them, our guide to [Christmas at school for Muslim families](/blog/christmas-at-school-muslim-families) offers age-by-age scripts.

Ask your local masjid whether it runs winter break programs for youth. Many Ottawa communities organize something, but schedules are usually only announced in December.

## Planning ahead for 2027

The winter break is short, and the next stretch of the year comes quickly.

- **Ramadan 2027** is expected to begin around February 8, with Eid al-Fitr around March 9 or 10, depending on moon sighting. The break is a good time to plan Ramadan crafts and a family goal.
- **March Break 2027** is March 15–19 for the Ottawa Catholic School Board. Camp registration often opens in January.
- **Kindergarten for September 2027:** Winter break is a good time to tour schools and compare [Islamic schools in Ottawa](/blog/islamic-schools-in-ottawa-guide), because registration timelines start early in the new year.

## A weekly anchor after the break

Many parents notice over the break how much their children enjoy time with other Muslim kids. If you want that every week, a [weekend Islamic school in Ottawa](/blog/weekend-islamic-schools-ottawa) is the simplest way to keep it going once school starts again.

For ages 8 to 15, QAF's [Sunday Quran and Arabic camp in Ottawa](/sunday-camp) meets Sundays from 11 AM to 3 PM at 900 Dynes Rd in Nepean. It covers Quran, Arabic reading and writing, math and STEM, and soccer, at $190 a month from September to June. For four- and five-year-olds, our full-day [Islamic JK/SK program](/program) runs 9 AM to 3 PM on weekdays, with daily Arabic immersion and Quran.
