Looking for an Islamic school near South Keys? As of October 2026 we could not find a full-time Islamic school inside South Keys, Hunt Club, Heron Gate or Alta Vista. You still have good options close by. QAF School's Islamic JK and SK at 900 Dynes Rd is about 6–7 km away, just across the Rideau River. Masjid ar-Rahmah on Hunt Club Rd runs evening and weekend Quran classes, and a Saturday Arabic and Islamic Studies program meets at Blossom Park Public School.

This guide shows what is actually within a short drive. It covers weekday and weekend options, how the commute works, and how to mix public school with Islamic learning if that suits you better.

> **Key takeaways**
> - Full-time Islamic kindergarten: QAF School (JK/SK, ages 4–5) at 900 Dynes Rd, about 10–15 minutes by car from South Keys outside rush hour.
> - Masjid ar-Rahmah (1216 Hunt Club Rd) runs the Albayan Quran schools on weekday evenings and Sunday mornings for ages 6 and up.
> - Iqraa Southkeys at Blossom Park Public School pairs a low-cost board-run Arabic class with a Quran and Islamic Studies block every Saturday.
> - Your designated public school depends on your exact address. Check the OCDSB and OCSB locators before you decide.
> - For ages 8–15, QAF Sunday Camp (Sundays 11 AM–3 PM) is close by, but it overlaps with Sunday-morning programs, so choose one.

## The commute from South Keys, Hunt Club, Heron Gate and Alta Vista

QAF sits on the west side of the Rideau River in Carleton Heights, near Prince of Wales Dr. From most of this area you cross the river at Hog's Back, so the drive is short even though the school is in a different ward.

Typical routes, measured on a map from each starting point:

- **South Keys Shopping Centre:** about 6.7 km. Take Hunt Club Rd west, then the Airport Parkway north to Brookfield Rd and Hog's Back Rd. About 10–15 minutes.
- **Heron Gate (Walkley and Heron area):** about 6 km via Heron Rd and Hog's Back Rd. About 10–15 minutes.
- **Alta Vista (around the Alta Vista library):** about 4.5 km via Heron Rd. About 8–12 minutes.
- **Hunt Club (around Bank St and Hunt Club Rd):** about 6–7 km via Hunt Club Rd and the Airport Parkway. About 10–15 minutes.

Morning traffic on Hunt Club Rd and Heron Rd can add several minutes. Leaving by 8:30 AM gets most families to QAF's 8:45–9:00 AM drop-off window without a rush.

**Transit:** the O-Train Line 2 reopened in January 2025 with stations at South Keys and Greenboro, according to [OC Transpo](https://www.octranspo.com/en/news/article/o-train-lines-2-and-4-open-january-6-2025/). The school is not right beside a station, though, so use OC Transpo's trip planner before you count on transit for a four-year-old's daily trip.

## Masjid ar-Rahmah and community programs

Masjid ar-Rahmah (Mosque of Mercy) at 1216 Hunt Club Rd is run by the Assunnah Muslims Association (AMA), which also runs the AMA Community Centre. For many families in South Keys and Hunt Club, it is the local masjid.

The AMA's published 2026/27 programs for children include:

- **Albayan School 1:** Quran reading, memorization and correct recitation, starting from the alphabet with the Bidaya book. Tuesdays and Thursdays, 6–8 PM, ages 6 to 16, in person. The listed fee is $425 per child for the year, supplies included.
- **Albayan School 2:** a Sunday Quran school, 10:30 AM to 1:00 PM, $300 per child for the year. It is for boys aged 6 to 9 and for girls from age 6.
- **Iqraa Southkeys:** on Saturdays at Blossom Park Public School. An OCDSB International Languages Arabic class runs from 9:30 AM to noon, then an AMA Islamic Studies and Quran memorization block runs from noon to 2:30 PM ($300 per year).
- **Toddlers Halaqa:** Islamic stories, crafts and skits for children aged 3 to 8, run as events. A parent stays on site.

Spaces fill up and dates change, so register early and check the masjid's site each summer. For a wider comparison of programs and prices across the city, see our guide to [Quran classes in Ottawa](/blog/quran-classes-for-kids-ottawa).

## Public school options in your area

All four Ottawa school boards serve this part of the city, and each one assigns a designated school by address. Two schools on the same street can belong to different boards, so check the tools rather than guessing:

- [OCDSB school locator](https://www.ocdsb.ca/our_schools/school_locator) (English public)
- [OCSB schools and locator](https://www.ocsb.ca/schools/) (English Catholic, which welcomes families of all faiths)
- CEPEO and CECCE for French-language schooling, if you are eligible

Local OCDSB schools include Alta Vista Public School, Blossom Park Public School and Featherston Drive Public School. [Featherston Drive](https://featherstondrps.ocdsb.ca/our-school-landing/about-us) says most of its students come from the Heron, Baycrest and Walkley Road triangle.

Board kindergarten is free, full-day, and in the English boards about half in French. What it does not include is Quran, Arabic or daily Islamic practice. Many families here choose a board school plus a weekend program. Others want faith and Arabic built into every day from the start, which is the gap a full-day Islamic kindergarten fills.

If you are weighing the boards against each other, [Ottawa's school boards compared](/blog/ottawa-school-boards-compared) explains eligibility, French and busing.

## A weekday Islamic kindergarten close to South Keys

For a four- or five-year-old, the main full-time Islamic option within a short drive is [our Islamic JK/SK program](/program) at 900 Dynes Rd. Here is what a South Keys family is actually signing up for:

- **Hours:** drop-off 8:45–9:00 AM, school day 9:00 AM–3:00 PM, pick-up 3:00–3:15 PM, Monday to Friday, September to June.
- **Learning:** a Montessori-inspired classroom with a long morning work cycle (9:20–11:00), aligned with Ontario's kindergarten curriculum.
- **Faith and language:** daily Arabic immersion, short surahs from Juz Amma, Islamic Studies, everyday duas and adab.
- **Practicalities:** families pack a halal, nut-free lunch and two snacks. Parents get daily photos, updates and teacher messages through ClassDojo.
- **Tuition:** $690 per month for 10 months plus a $190 annual resource fee ($7,090 per year), with a sibling discount.

The commute matters at this age, so do one test drive at 8:30 AM on a weekday before you commit. Weigh it against what you gain: a school day that already includes Quran and Arabic frees up evenings and weekends for rest and family time.

## Weekends for older kids: choose, don't stack

Once children are in school full-time, weekends become the main place for Quran and Arabic. South Keys families have more choice here than most of the city, which brings its own risk: signing up for everything.

A realistic plan for ages 8 to 15:

1. **Pick one main Quran program.** Albayan's weekday evenings suit kids who are fine with a late dinner twice a week. A Sunday program suits families who want weekday evenings free.
2. **Add Arabic only if it is a separate goal.** The Saturday board-run Arabic class is low-cost and close by.
3. **Watch the clock on Sundays.** Albayan School 2 (10:30 AM–1:00 PM) overlaps with QAF Sunday Camp (11 AM–3 PM), so you cannot do both.

[QAF Sunday Camp](/sunday-camp) runs every Sunday from 11 AM to 3 PM at 900 Dynes Rd for ages 8 to 15, in junior and senior classes. It covers Quran, Arabic reading and writing, math and STEM, and soccer, for $190 per month from September to June. It suits kids who need Quran and Arabic and also need to move. For more weekend options across the city, see [weekend Islamic schools in Ottawa](/blog/weekend-islamic-schools-ottawa).

## Family life around South Keys and Alta Vista

Islamic education also happens outside classrooms. A few local places that make family routines easier:

- **Libraries:** the Alta Vista branch on Alta Vista Dr and the Greenboro branch on Lorry Greenberg Dr. Both are good for storytimes and for borrowing Muslim picture books on hold from the [Ottawa Public Library](https://biblioottawalibrary.ca).
- **Parks and water:** Mooney's Bay Park has a beach and a large playground. Hog's Back Park and its falls sit right on the route between Dynes Rd and Alta Vista, which makes them an easy after-school stop.
- **Recreation:** the Jim Durrell Recreation Centre on Walkley Rd has arenas, and community centres across the area post seasonal programs on ottawa.ca.
- **Masjid life:** taking children to Jumu'ah on PA days or to an evening halaqa at Masjid ar-Rahmah gives the weekend program a home.

## Is QAF right for your family?

QAF is a good fit for South Keys, Hunt Club, Heron Gate and Alta Vista families who have a child turning four or five, can drive about 10–15 minutes each way, and want Quran, Arabic and Islamic manners woven into a full school day rather than added on at the weekend. It also suits parents who like a calm, Montessori-style classroom and daily updates through ClassDojo.

It is not the right fit for everyone. We only offer JK and SK, so you will choose a Grade 1 school after two years. If you want one building from kindergarten to Grade 8, a board school or a full-time Islamic elementary school may suit you better. If your child is 6 or 7, our Sunday Camp starts at 8, so a local program such as Albayan may fit sooner.
