The best PA day activities in Ottawa are the ones that fit a single, often chilly, weekday: a museum in the morning, a public swim or skate in the afternoon, a day camp if you are working, or a slow day at home with a library haul. This guide lists the 2026–27 PA days for Ottawa's largest board, ideas matched to each date's season, and a few ways to give the day an Islamic touch, including the fact that most of this year's remaining PA days fall on a Friday.

> **Key takeaways**
> - OCDSB elementary PA days left in 2026–27: November 20 and 23, 2026, and January 29, April 16 and June 4, 2027. Check your own board's calendar.
> - Four of those five days are Fridays, which makes a family trip to Jumu'ah an easy and meaningful PA day plan.
> - If you are working, book a PA day camp early. The YMCA's 2026 PA day camps cost about $70 a day for non-members and filled quickly.
> - Weekday mornings are the quietest time at Ottawa's museums, pools and indoor play spaces.

## Ottawa PA days in 2026–27

A PA day (professional activity day) is a school day when students stay home while teachers attend training or work on assessment. Ontario regulation sets how many a board may hold each year, and each board chooses its own dates.

According to the [OCDSB 2026–2027 elementary calendar](https://www.ocdsb.ca/download/556470), the remaining elementary PA days are:

| Date | Day | Season | What makes it different |
|---|---|---|---|
| November 20, 2026 | Friday | Late fall | Short daylight; Jumu'ah day |
| November 23, 2026 | Monday | Late fall | Long weekend for elementary students; many museums close Mondays |
| January 29, 2027 | Friday | Deep winter | Skating and snow day; falls between semesters for secondary |
| April 16, 2027 | Friday | Spring | Muddy trails, maple season winding down |
| June 4, 2027 | Friday | Early summer | Outdoor day: splash pads, parks, Gatineau Park |

OCDSB secondary students have slightly different PA days: November 23, January 29, April 16 and June 24–25 (see the [secondary calendar](https://www.ocdsb.ca/download/556471)). The Ottawa Catholic School Board, CEPEO, CECCE and private schools publish their own dates, so check your child's school before you plan.

## PA day activities in Ottawa by type

### Museums: plan around opening days

Ottawa's national museums are made for weekday visits, when crowds are smaller. The catch: several close one or two weekdays in the off-season. The Canada Science and Technology Museum and Canada Agriculture and Food Museum close on Mondays, and the Canadian Museum of Nature closes Tuesdays in its winter season, so double-check hours before the **Monday, November 23** PA day.

For a Friday PA day, the Science and Technology Museum or the Museum of Nature make a full morning. Our guide to [things to do with kids in Ottawa](/blog/things-to-do-with-kids-ottawa) covers free admission hours, and the Ottawa Public Library lends museum passes too.

### Swimming, skating and sports

The City of Ottawa runs public swims and skates at its pools and arenas. Look up times and book any reserved sessions through the City's recreation site, [register.ottawa.ca](https://register.ottawa.ca), a few days ahead.

On **January 29**, if the weather is cold enough, the Rideau Canal Skateway or a neighbourhood outdoor rink is a classic PA day outing. For girls who prefer a private swim, ask your local pool whether it runs women-only or family swim times, and bring a modest swimsuit or rash guard.

### Library and maker programs

The [Ottawa Public Library](https://biblioottawalibrary.ca/en) is the most underrated PA day plan in the city: free, warm and close to home. Check your branch's events calendar for children's programs, borrow a stack of books and audiobooks for the weekend, and ask about museum passes while you are there. A good library haul can turn a PA day into a cozy reading afternoon.

### Outdoors

- **November:** A short walk in Gatineau Park or along a local ravine trail before the snow, then hot chocolate.
- **January:** Tobogganing at a neighbourhood hill or a snowshoe on a groomed trail.
- **April:** Puddle walks and the last of maple season; dress for mud.
- **June:** City splash pads, a beach day if it is warm, or a long bike ride on the Experimental Farm pathways.

### Indoor play for the coldest days

Trampoline parks, indoor playgrounds and climbing gyms are busiest on weekends and PA days alike, so book a morning slot if you can. Ask about grip socks and age limits before you go.

## PA day camps if you are working

PA days are hardest for working parents. These are the main options.

1. **School-based care.** If your child is in a before- and after-school program, ask whether it runs on PA days and how to register. Some programs require separate PA day registration well in advance.
2. **YMCA PA day camps.** The YMCA of the National Capital Region runs [PA day camps](https://www.ymcaottawa.ca/programs-services/camps/school-break-camps/pa-day-camps) for ages 4 to 12, from 8 AM to 5 PM. In 2026 they cost $70 for non-members and $63 for members, on set dates at specific locations. Check that their dates match your board's PA days.
3. **Recreation and sports facilities.** Community centres, gymnastics clubs, martial arts schools and swim clubs often run single-day PA day camps. Search the City's registration site and your child's activity providers.
4. **Masjid programs.** Few Ottawa mosques run PA day camps, but some run youth programs on Friday evenings and weekends. Our guide to [Muslim youth programs in Ottawa](/blog/muslim-youth-programs-ottawa) lists them by area.
5. **A parent swap.** Two or three families take turns hosting on PA days. It is free, kids love it, and it is easy to keep halal.

## Giving a PA day an Islamic touch

### Take the kids to Jumu'ah

Four of the five remaining OCDSB elementary PA days are Fridays: **November 20, January 29, April 16 and June 4.** For many children in public school, this is the only chance all year to attend Jumu'ah prayer with a parent. Each masjid sets its own Jumu'ah time, so check your local mosque's schedule; some hold more than one Jumu'ah.

Make it special. Walk in together, sit together, and go for lunch afterwards. Our guide to [teaching kids salah](/blog/teach-kids-salah) has tips for young children in the masjid.

### A mini Quran morning

A slower day is a good time for a surah your child has been stuck on. Try a 20-minute session after breakfast: listen to the surah twice, recite it together, then let your child "teach" it to a younger sibling. Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas are good places to start.

### Prayer times on PA days

In late November, daylight is short: in Ottawa, Dhuhr falls just before noon, Asr in the early afternoon and Maghrib in the late afternoon, around 4:30 PM. A museum or skating outing can easily include two prayers, so pack a travel mat and check the times before you leave. By April and June, Dhuhr is around 1 PM and the afternoon is long.

### Give back

A PA day is a good time for a small act of sadaqah: baking for a neighbour, sorting toys to donate, or helping at a masjid food program. Let your child choose which one.

## A simple PA day plan

If you only have a few minutes to plan, use this outline.

1. **Morning:** one outing (museum, library, pool or park), booked or checked the night before.
2. **Midday:** Dhuhr (or Jumu'ah on a Friday) and a packed halal lunch.
3. **Afternoon:** quiet time at home: reading, Lego, a craft or a 20-minute Quran review.
4. **Late afternoon:** outdoor play before Maghrib, especially in winter when it gets dark early.

Keep screens for the last hour if at all. Our list of [screen-free weekend activities](/blog/screen-free-weekend-ideas) works just as well on a PA day.
